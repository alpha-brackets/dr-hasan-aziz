01The doctor

A specialist who listens first.

Dr. Hasan Aziz is a consultant physician at Dua Hospital and Hayat Medical Center, Multan. He treats adults for everyday illness, such as fever, cough and infections, and for long-term conditions, such as diabetes and high blood pressure.

If you need a surgeon or another specialist, he explains why and guides you to the right one.

06Before your visit

Questions patients ask

Still unsure? Send a WhatsApp message and someone will answer.

Emergency? Don't wait for a message.Chest pain, fainting or trouble breathing: call 1122 or go to the nearest emergency.
How do I book?

Send a WhatsApp message with your name and problem. The clinic replies with your time and which clinic to visit.

Which days is he available?

Monday to Saturday. Hayat Medical Center 11 AM – 7 PM, Dua Hospital 7 – 10 PM. Both clinics are closed on Sunday.

Can I come without booking?

To confirm with the clinic. Booking first means less waiting.

What should I bring?

Old prescriptions, test reports and the medicines you take now. Sugar patients: bring your readings.

What is the fee?

To confirm with the clinic. Ask in your WhatsApp message.

Which clinic should I choose?

Same doctor at both. Choose Hayat for daytime, Dua Hospital for evenings.
